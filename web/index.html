Terrarium

A small world, and the people who try to live in it. They forage, share, quarrel, build, fall ill and raise children. You watch.

    What happens

    Every day each person looks around, weighs hunger, kin, friends and grudges, and acts: gather, eat, rest, share food, court, build a shelter, claim land, or take what they need. Seasons turn, droughts and storms come without warning, illness spreads between neighbours.

    How it runs

    The simulation engine is written in Rust and compiled to WebAssembly; it runs entirely on this page. Without a language model, people act on instinct: rules taken from behavioural ecology rather than a script.
